Privacy Policy

Draft — pending legal review — last updated 30 July 2026

Who we are: Melb Twenty is a Melbourne-based location matching app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights under Australian privacy law.

What we collect: your email; profile details (name, gender, bio, photos, interests); sensitive information you choose to share (sexual orientation, relationship style and structure, including any kink tags) — collected only with your consent; your precise location (used only for distance-based matching — other users only ever see an approximate distance, never your exact location); and usage data like likes, passes, blocks, matches, and messages.

How it's used: to power matching, let you message your matches, keep the app safe (reviewing reports, moderating photos, and reviewing message content when investigating a report or suspected abuse), and improve the app. We do not sell your information or use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with: only the service providers that run the app (Supabase for hosting/database, Vercel for web hosting) — data is hosted in Sydney, Australia. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Security: passwords are hashed, data access is restricted per-user, and all traffic is encrypted. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted — we can access message content where necessary for safety or legal reasons, and we don't otherwise read or monitor your messages.

Your rights: access, correct, or delete your data anytime, and complain about how we've handled it — including to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) if unresolved. Contact us below to act on any of these.

Age: Melb Twenty is 18+ only. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Questions? Contact our privacy team.

Terms of Service

Draft — pending legal review — last updated 30 July 2026

Eligibility: you must be 18 or older, and may only hold one account with accurate information about yourself.

What Melb Twenty is: a location-based dating app where you set your side of town, orientation, intent, relationship style and structure, and discover, like, match, and message nearby users.

Conduct: no harassment, impersonation, non-consensual sharing of images, or illegal activity. Content involving, sexualising, or depicting a minor in any form is never permitted and will be reported to authorities.

Your safety: we don't run background checks on users — use your own judgment, meet in public places, and tell someone where you're going. Report anything concerning.

Your content: you keep ownership of what you post; you're giving us permission to store and display it to run the app, and you confirm you have the right to share it.

Enforcement: we review reports and can warn, suspend, or remove any account that violates these terms, without notice in serious cases.

Liability: the app is provided as-is with no guaranteed outcomes, and Melb Twenty isn't liable for your interactions with other users, to the extent permitted by Australian law.

Governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia. Questions? Contact us.
